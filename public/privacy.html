Readback

Privacy

Last updated 26 November 2026.

What stays on your device

The request you paste is decoded in your browser. The verdict and findings come from rules that run locally. Readback has no accounts, no analytics and no database, and it never asks you to connect a wallet.

When AI is used

Readback asks Gemini to explain the decoded facts through Firebase AI Logic. In Chrome on desktop with Gemini Nano, the model runs on your device and nothing is sent anywhere.

If the on-device model is not available, the request is long, or you choose a language Gemini Nano can’t write yet, Readback can use Google’s cloud-hosted Gemini model instead. Before that happens, every address and long hex value is replaced with a placeholder such as [SPENDER]. If you fill in the optional site field, that site name is included, because the explanation uses it. Below each answer you can open exactly what was sent. You can turn cloud fallback off.

Cloud requests are handled by Google under the Firebase terms and Google’s privacy policy. Firebase App Check uses reCAPTCHA to confirm requests come from this app.

Stored in your browser

Only two preferences: your explanation language and whether cloud fallback is on. Clear your site data to remove them.
